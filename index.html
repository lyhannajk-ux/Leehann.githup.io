About

N° 002 — Portrait, Studio

I've spent the last twelve years photographing the in-between hours — the ones before a city wakes up or after a coastline empties out. Most of the work starts with a 6x7 medium-format camera, a tripod, and no real plan beyond staying somewhere until the light does something worth keeping.

Before photography I trained as a printmaker, which is probably why I still care more about tone and grain than resolution. Everything here is shot on film and scanned by hand, then printed in small runs for gallery shows and private collections across Europe and North America.

When I'm not travelling for a project, I run a two-day analog printing workshop out of a converted boathouse north of the city — details on request.
